## ☁️ Google Cloud AI & Developer Relations

As a **Developer Advocate at Google**, I focus on making complex AI technologies accessible to developers worldwide.

*   **Generative AI & LLMs:** Lead contributor and reviewer for the `GoogleCloudPlatform/generative-ai` repository. I've created numerous tutorials, demos, and Jupyter notebooks for Generative AI on Vertex AI.
*   **Protocols & SDKs:** Primary maintainer for the **Agent2Agent (A2A) Protocol**. I also maintain the **Document AI Toolbox SDK** and have developed key features for the Vertex AI and Google Gen AI SDKs.
*   **Community Engagement:** Presented at **Google Cloud Next** and **Google I/O**. My technical content and talks have been viewed by over 0.5 million developers globally.
*   **Open Source:** Creator of multiple **LangChain Google connectors** and a key collaborator on Vertex AI documentation.
